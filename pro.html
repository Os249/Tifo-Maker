Questions

Does anything free become paid?

No. The editor, 3D, Match Day, the PDF, CSV and QR, saving and publishing stay free. Pro adds more on top.

Does a pass renew by itself?

No. A pass ends on its own and nothing is charged again.

What happens to my designs when Pro ends?

They stay yours, as they are. Only new exports with Pro items need Pro again.

Where does my picture go for a smart cut-out?

To Cloudflare's AI, which cuts it out and sends it back. We do not keep it.

How will I pay?

With mada, Apple Pay or STC Pay, when passes go on sale.
