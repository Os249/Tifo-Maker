TifoMaker Pro

Everything for the big match

Everything free stays free. Pro adds Match Day films in Full HD and 9:16, a 4K poster, exports without the mark, portrait styles, Pro fonts, your club kit and more.

Questions

Does anything free become paid?

No. The editor, 3D, Match Day, the PDF, CSV and QR, saving and publishing stay free. Pro adds more on top.

Does Pro renew by itself?

No. A month of Pro ends on its own and nothing is charged again.

What happens to my designs when Pro ends?

They stay yours, as they are. Only new exports with Pro items need Pro again.

Where does my picture go for a smart cut-out?

To Cloudflare's AI, which cuts it out and sends it back. We do not keep it.

How will I pay?

With mada, Apple Pay or STC Pay, when Pro goes on sale.

Does the 7-day gift run out if I wait?

No. It waits until you press Start, and the 7 days begin then. One gift per account.
