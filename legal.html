Privacy

I try to collect as little as possible. This explains what I collect and why, in line with the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) and similar good practice.

Who's responsible

TifoMaker is run by one individual developer. For any privacy question or request, email osamah9070@gmail.com.

What I collect

  • If you make an account: your email address, a username, and a securely hashed password (I never see your actual password).
  • What you create: the designs and any text or images you make or upload.
  • Audience measurement (no cookie, no consent needed): for each page view I store a small set of non‑identifying facts: which site or search engine linked you here (the website name only, never the full address or anything you searched for), any campaign tag in the link, the page you opened, whether you're on a phone or a computer, your browser's main language, and your country when my hosting provides it. Your IP address is never stored. It is combined once, in memory, with a secret that is regenerated every day and never written to disk, to produce a one‑way code that lets me count returning visits within a single day. Because the secret changes daily and is never saved, that code cannot be turned back into you, and it cannot be used to follow you from one day to the next. The code is deleted entirely after two days, leaving only totals. This sets nothing on your device, so there is nothing here to consent to, and it is used only to understand where visitors come from. Never advertising, never profiling, never shared or sold.
  • Usage analytics (only if you agree): if you choose "Accept all" in the cookie banner, the site also records which steps of the editor a browsing session reached, so I can see where people get stuck. Choose "Essential only" and none of this runs.
  • Basic technical logs: my hosting provider records ordinary web‑server logs, which include IP address and browser details, for a short period. These keep the site running and secure (spotting outages and blocking abuse) and are not used for analytics.

Why I use it

To run the site and your account, verify your email, keep accounts secure, prevent abuse, and send you important account emails (verification, password reset). Separately, to understand how many people visit and where they come from, so I know whether the site is worth continuing: that part uses no cookie and no personal data, as described above, and it is never used for advertising, profiling or personalisation. The extra in‑editor usage stats run only if you consent.

Who else is involved

A few trusted services help run TifoMaker and may process some data on my behalf: web hosting, an email service (for verification and password‑reset messages), and the AI provider (which processes your prompts when you use the AI Designer). There is no third‑party analytics company, no Google Analytics, no advertising or tracking pixels of any kind. All measurement described above happens on my own server and the results go nowhere else. I don't sell your data to anyone.
