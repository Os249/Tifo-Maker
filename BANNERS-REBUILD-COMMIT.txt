=== SUMMARY ===

Banners: review the whole system, and fix what a user would hit


=== DESCRIPTION ===

Your main is at "Tifo Maker — banners rebuild". This commit is everything since: the review of the whole banner system, which is most of it; last round's fix for the seats showing through the fabric, which reached your folder after that commit; the stadium panel's lost strings put back, with three things the stadium changes had exposed; and the five retired banner files emptied, so that main builds again.

THE REVIEW: EVERYTHING A USER WOULD HIT

The whole system was driven the way a person drives it — desktop, laptop, Arabic, phone, the editor's bowl and Match Day — and every state photographed. Most of what that found was invisible to every existing test, because each thing was correct in the state the tests put it in and broken in the state a person arrives at.

Broken:

- The block and tier pickers were never on screen. The panel asked Match Day for them by an event only an open simulator answered — and Match Day covers the whole screen, so nobody could ever have seen them. The panel builds the same stand frames Match Day does now, from the same seat map, through one cache every caller shares, and the size it shows is measured on the ground rather than "about".
- Ctrl+Z in the Banner view undid the SEAT design, behind the artboard, where nothing on screen changed. Delete deleted the seat editor's selection. The keys go to the banner now: undo and redo, Delete, Ctrl+D, the arrows to nudge, Escape to let go.
- There was no way to take one thing off a banner. A word in the wrong place meant Undo, or "Clear the art". A selected item gets Front, Back, Duplicate and Delete in the panel.
- In Match Day, choosing East put the banner on the North stand: East is stand zero, and `Number('0') || 1` is one.
- A new banner was see-through with nothing on it, which draws nothing — make a banner, look for it on the stand, find nothing. It starts as white cloth now; one stored empty and see-through comes back as cloth; one see-through with art on it is kept, and the panel says only the art will show.
- A hanging banner between two tiers squashed its artwork about twelve times over, a 2:1 design stretched onto an 11:1 strip. That slot imposes the gap's shape, so the shape is kept beside the user's own: the artboard becomes the strip, the texture is drawn at it with enough pixels across its short side, the shape controls lock and say why, and moving the banner out of the gap gives the user's shape back untouched.
- The texture ignored the sheet's shape, so after a shape change the bowl kept drawing the old picture on the new sheet.
- "See it on match day" opened on the default camera with the banner behind it, and the camera glide that starts on opening then undid "Look at it". It opens on the banner now, with the Banners section open.

Misleading:

- Every reveal was offered to both types, and the geometry has one motion each: "Haul up on ropes" on a stand banner unrolled it. Each type offers its own motion or "Already up", and "Already up" no longer runs an animation of a thing it says does not happen.
- The reveal's length was fixed when the banner was made and went stale with every size change, then reported the user as having chosen it. It follows the real banner until the slider is moved, and "Use the real time" puts it back.
- Changing type re-proportioned the sheet under the art and dropped the Size menu to Custom.
- Seat-only controls were all over the Banner view: a second brush slider that did nothing to the banner, Stadium fills, the card-flip reveal, card logistics, the seat minimap, "Height 18 seats" and "click a stand to place".
- Placement was blind — the panel in one view, the bowl in another. "Show it in the stadium", or Split from a banner, puts the bowl beside the artboard and turns it to look wherever the banner goes. The Stadium view gets a "Your banner" camera.
- The artboard jumped half a tool bar whenever the text, shape or import bar opened, so a word landed away from where it was clicked. Things placed on a strip hung off its hem.
- A second banner landed exactly on top of the first. Undo could delete the banner the editor made by itself. A banner hidden in Match Day could not be un-hidden anywhere else. Banners could not be renamed. Match Day's scrub bar sat at the end while a minute-long haul played.

And the smaller things: white fabric blew out to a flat white shape in the bowl; the guides vanished on white; the legible-type band was unlabelled; "Across" was clipped to "A"; the tool bar split "Fabric" from its weight; two different things were called "Size"; the two stands of a corner run were not named; Match Day listed stands in another order and its help sent banners to Tifo Assets.

THE SEATS THROUGH THE FABRIC

A banner is held off the terracing by the crowd, and that support faded to zero as the stand emptied — but a card is drawn at every seat whether anyone is in it or not, and its top is 1.05 m out along the banner's normal. On an empty stand the sheet sat 0.4 m off the concrete and thirty thousand cards came straight through it. The editor's own bowl has no crowd at all, so it was the worst case in the product. The support runs from the cards to the heads now. The sweep runs empty stands as well as full ones (104,504 configurations), and the pixel gate uses the closest camera, because from 245 m up the whole defect was smaller than a pixel.

THE STADIUM PANEL

Every banner delivery wrote the whole of src/ui/i18n.ts, and each one after the stadium session's put an older copy back. That dropped fourteen of its strings and the new wording of three more. On screen today it is the stadium panel's region filter reading "sp.anyRegion" and every stadium's details reading "sp.region"; the other fifteen belong to the Custom tab — the OpenStreetMap import and saving a stadium — and would have printed their keys at the user the moment Custom was switched back on. All seventeen are back word for word from your own history (commit 0b767f5), in both languages. `npm run verify` is what found it — it checks that every key the stadium fitter can emit has a string — and it reads one entry per line, so si.help is on one line now.

With the stadium work in the test pass for the first time, three more things showed, all from putting the ten real-venue grounds on the tab everyone opens ("Show all 13 stadiums"). Nobody saw them while those grounds sat on the Community tab:

- Ten of the thirteen stadiums had no name in either language. The list printed their English names in Arabic, and the status bar and the share page — which print tl(template.id) — printed the raw id, "community-jewel-jeddah-62k", in both. They have names in both languages now, and `npm run verify` fails if a shipped stadium does not.
- The notice under a ground built from a real venue was a hard-coded English string, so an Arabic user picking the Jewel of Jeddah, Al-Awwal Park or Kingdom Arena read the one legal line on the panel in English. It is translated now, with the same substance as the legal page's non-affiliation line. It also no longer opens with "Community-created", which stopped being true when those ten were retagged built-in — worth a look, since it is legal wording.
- The dropdown chevron the stadium session brought back sat on top of the filter text: those selects had an even 6 px of padding, and in Arabic, where the text starts at the right, the arrow landed between the first two words. They leave it room now.

e2e-i18n has a new surface for this — the panel with a real-venue ground loaded — because the default ground is not one, which is how the names and the notice went unchecked. Against the tree before these fixes it finds twelve English strings; after, none.

WHY MAIN DID NOT BUILD

The last commit included the five retired banner files — the cloth solver, its heightfield, bannerFit, bannerMigrate and banner-physics. They no longer compile, and `npm run build` typechecks everything, so main as committed fails it, and so does the Docker build Railway runs (`npm run build:prod` is the same command). They are emptied here — a comment and `export {}` — so it builds whether or not they are deleted; deleting them is still the right end state. `npm run probe:banner`, which ran one of them, is removed from package.json.

TWO TESTS THAT FAILED FOR THE WRONG REASON

- e2e-seo failed on main as well as here: its test account used a password the strong-password rule rejects, so the registration failed and every check after it. It uses a strong one now.
- e2e-mobile's two-finger-undo check read the toast exactly 900 ms after the gesture, and the toast lives exactly 900 ms. On a loaded machine it lost the race and failed with the toast's own text, "Undone", in hand. It watches for the toast now.
