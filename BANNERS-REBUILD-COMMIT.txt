=== SUMMARY ===

Banners: announce them, add a tour and a showcase, and free Match Day's camera


=== DESCRIPTION ===

Four things before banners go out: tell people they exist, teach the Banner view the first time it opens, put some in the community, and let Match Day's camera move again after "See it on match day".

1. MATCH DAY: THE CAMERA WAS STUCK

"See it on match day" opened on the banner, and then the camera would not move. There were two causes.

- The controls were switched off. Opening the simulator starts a camera glide and turns the controls off until it lands. Pointing at the banner cancelled the glide, so it never landed and the controls never came back. No orbit, no zoom, no pan for the rest of the visit. They come back now.
- Every drag landed on the banner. The shot frames the banner across most of the picture, and pressing on a banner took hold of it. So nearly every attempt to look around slid the banner along the stand instead.

Now the first click on a banner selects it (the outline comes up and the panel follows) and leaves the camera alone. Dragging moves the banner only once it is selected. Pressing anywhere else orbits the camera and deselects the banner. It opens from the Banner view with nothing selected. The panel hint says how it works: "Click a banner to pick it out, then drag it to move it along the stand."

2. "BANNERS ARE HERE"

Everyone who opens the editor gets a short card just under the Banner tab, pointing at it: "Banners are here. Draw a stand banner or a hanging banner, put it on any stand, and watch it unroll on match day." It has two buttons, Try banners and Not now.

- It sits beside the work, not in front of it. There is no backdrop, and it does not take focus.
- It waits its turn behind the first-visit dialog, a tour and Match Day, and never stacks on top of them.
- It goes away on Try, Not now, the X or Escape, or when you switch view. Leaving it open would put it over the Stadium view's camera bar.
- It is remembered once answered. A reload before that shows it again.
- On a phone it points at the view pill's Banner button. English and Arabic.

3. THE BANNER TOUR

The first time anyone opens the Banner view, it runs its own short tour. Skip, Escape and Back work on every step. "How banners work" in the Banners panel runs it again. It follows the state of the tifo:

- No banner yet: what a banner is, then making one, the list, and where to find the tour again.
- With a banner: the sheet (type, size, fabric), the drawing tools, the list and Show in stadium, placement, the reveal and "See it on match day", and where to find the tour again.
- Phone: three steps. The Banner view, the sheet (or making one), and where its settings are (Paint).

Steps whose controls are not on the screen are left out. At 1024 wide the panel is a closed slide-over, and the tour no longer points past the edge of the window.

This one is shown automatically and the main tour is not (see the note in main.ts). The difference is timing: it comes when someone has just opened a view that works differently from the rest of the editor.

The tour code itself had four bugs, fixed here:
- A tour that ended on a button left its Escape listener behind, so the next tour ended on its first Escape.
- A step could point at a control past the edge of the window.
- In Arabic, "beside the panel" put the card on the wrong side.
- The card could land on top of the thing it was describing.

4. THE COMMUNITY: THREE STADIUMS WITH DIFFERENT BANNERS

The community feed now opens on three designs by @tifomaker, one per ground, each with different banners:
- Generic 60k bowl: a hanging banner flown from the roof, green, with a crest and «دائماً معك».
- Grand oval: a four-block stand banner unrolled over the North stand, "WE ARE THE NORTH".
- Single-tier kop: two banners. A see-through mesh stand banner ("ONE CLUB / ONE CITY") and a tall one-block hanging "12".

The seats are three of the library's own designs, which have already passed its quality gates. The banners use each design's palette.

They are published the same way as the starter library: by @tifomaker, flagged as templates. So they carry the Template badge, stay out of "Made by people", can never be Tifo of the day, and are never passed off as someone's post. scripts/generate-showcase.mts writes server/data/showcase.jsonl. The server publishes it at boot after the library, once, by title.

To make them worth opening, three things that had never worked with banners now do:
- The community preview shows a design's banners. It showed the seats only, so any design saved with a banner appeared there without it. Designs with banners open on the banner, and the camera bar has a button for each one.
- Cards say "With banners". The card strip only shows the seats.
- Remix and fork take the banners with them. They copied the seats and nothing else, so remixing a tifo for its banner gave you a copy with no banner.

SMALLER FIXES

- At 768 to 1099 px the closed slide-over panel could still be focused. Tab, a tour or a click into it scrolled the whole page 312 px sideways, header and all, with no way back. It is hidden once it has slid out.
- In the community preview, a long title ran under the close button.
