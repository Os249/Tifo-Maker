=== SUMMARY ===

Banners: add signs, the ultras' message held up by a row of fans


=== DESCRIPTION ===

A third banner type, Sign. It is the long, narrow sheet with one line painted on it that a curva holds up to say something to the rest of the ground: "IF YOU CAN'T SEE WHO IS THE LEADER EAT MORE CARROTS", "ON EXIGE UN RECRUTEMENT", "12 سنة من سوء التدبير". Italians call it a striscione, the DFB glossary a Spruchband. It is not a stand banner or a hanging banner, so it gets its own placement and sizing, but it reuses everything else banners already have.

1. WHAT A SIGN IS IN THE APP

- Type "Sign" in the Banner view, next to Stand banner and Hanging banner. The empty state and the add row both offer it.
- It is sized in metres, like the real sheet. Length is 4 to 40 m and height 0.8 to 2 m. A new one is 12 x 1.2 m.
- It is placed where the people holding it stand:
  - Held at: the fence (lowest tier), the balcony (upper tiers), the front row, or any row further back.
  - Along: the middle of the stand, the middle of any block, or any aisle.
- In the front row it is held at chest height. Further back it is held up over the heads of the row in front, measured from the stand's real rake. On the fence or balcony it is tied on and hangs down in front.
- A Message field sits at the top of the panel, with font and colour. The line is fitted to the sheet and refitted whenever the text, length or height changes. Typing is one undo step.
- Facts: "held by 9 fans · 1.6 kg · letters up to 0.9 m", or "tied to the fence". The notes say how far its letters read (signage rule, about 90 m per metre of letter height) and suggest a taller sheet when it falls short of the far side. No seams across it, because the fabric runs along its length.
- Reveal "Raised by the crowd": it goes up from its bottom edge in 1–3 s. It can also already be up (cut).

2. IN THE STADIUM

- An upright sheet along its row, standing in front of its holders. It sags a little between hands, the hands bob out of step, and the free bottom hem moves in the wind. A tied sign unrolls down from the fence.
- Show in stadium and Match Day's "Look at it" frame it from across the pitch, close enough to read it.
- Match Day: a sign shows Held at and Along in place of Across, Blocks and Span. Dragging it moves it to the row and place under the pointer.
- Where the ground cannot take what was asked, the ground wins, never the sheet:
  - The balcony is only offered where there is a drop to the tier below. On the cauldron dome the tiers run into each other, and its first-row signs are held over the heads of the tier below.
  - A sheet deeper than a short balcony stands up above the rail. It is not squashed.
  - A balcony sign stands clear of a fascia that leans out under the lip.
  - Under an overhang, only the rows where a sheet that tall can be held up are offered. On the wide oval that removes the last two or three rows of the lower tier.
