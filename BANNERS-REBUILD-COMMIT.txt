=== SUMMARY ===

Banners: a sheet across two stands, and rope that arrives somewhere


=== DESCRIPTION ===

A BANNER CAN STRETCH ACROSS TWO STANDS NOW, carrying on round the corner into the next one — the same sheet, laced across the join.

It is not a special case, and that is the point. Every measurement in a stand frame was already relative to the window it was built over; nothing in it actually cared that the window was a quarter of the bowl. So the frame is now built over a span, the per-stand frame is a one-stand call of it, and the blocks, tiers, widths, rake and normals of a two-stand window follow with no further work. A banner picks its window with `slot.stands`, and the column count scales with it so the resolution per metre of stand is the same either way.

On the generic bowl one stand is eight blocks and 134 m; two are fifteen blocks and 268 m — fifteen rather than sixteen because the corner's two half blocks are one block once the join is inside the window, which is what they are on the ground.

Changing the window re-centres the run rather than keeping its index: block 3 of a stand and block 3 of a pair are different places, and a banner that silently moved somewhere else would be the worse answer.

A FLOWN BANNER'S ROPES ARRIVE SOMEWHERE. They ran straight up in world Y from the sheet's corners to the roof's HEIGHT at the sheet's own position — a line to a point in empty air, which from most angles crossed the terracing and read as two stray threads over the seats. They are tied to the leading edge of the roof now, which makes them lean back the way real rigging does, and to the rail at the back of the stand on a ground with no roof over that side, because a rope to a roof that is not there is a thread ending in mid-air. The simulator answers where, since only it knows the template's roof coverage.

They are also geometry rather than `LineSegments`. A line is one pixel wide at any distance, which in a stadium reads as a scratch on the lens; these are thin cylinders that catch the floodlights and get thinner with distance like everything else.

The sweep now enumerates both windows — every stand on its own, and every stand paired with the one round the corner from it. That is 86,528 configurations. The two-stand half is checked at the worst case only, full wind and fully revealed, to keep the whole run inside a few minutes.
