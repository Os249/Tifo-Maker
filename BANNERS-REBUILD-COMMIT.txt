=== SUMMARY ===

Banners: a tifo starts with none, and each one has Show in stadium


=== DESCRIPTION ===

A brand-new tifo opened with a banner already on it. There were three separate reasons, and one of them was my last commit.

1. THE BANNER VIEW MADE ONE BY ITSELF

The first time the Banner view opened, it put a banner in the tifo, and that banner was saved like any other. So anyone who only LOOKED at the view had a banner on their North stand from then on. Not everyone wants a banner. The view no longer makes one. With none, the artboard says what a banner is and makes either kind in one click, and the sheet's controls stay out of the way until there is a banner to set up.

2. MY LAST COMMIT TURNED THOSE INTO WHITE SHEETS

Last round, a stored see-through banner with nothing on it came back as white cloth. The idea was to make an invisible banner findable. But nearly every banner like that was the one the view had made by itself, so the change put a white sheet on every tifo that had ever opened the Banner view. Stored banners now come back exactly as they were stored. The one nobody made (nothing drawn, no fabric, every setting still what a new banner starts with) is dropped when it loads. Change anything about it and it counts as somebody's banner, and it stays.

3. BANNERS WERE NOT TIED TO THEIR TIFO

Banners live in their own key in the browser, and they were loaded whatever the canvas held. A first visit, a link to another stadium, an imported file, a shared design and a design opened from the gallery all came up with the last tifo's banners. A gallery design also kept them on its next save.

Now:
- Banners come back only with their own tifo: the draft being restored, or the same design moved to another stadium.
- The banner key is written every time the draft is, so the two always describe the same tifo. A banner-only tifo is kept as a draft too.
- A design opened from the gallery, or a .tifo file, gets its own banners or none.
- If a design's banners fail to load, saving it no longer writes "none" over them.

SHOW IN STADIUM

The Banner view's panel lists every banner in the tifo, showing its fabric colour, name, type and stand. Each banner has its own "Show in stadium" button, which goes straight to the Stadium view with the camera on that banner, and says so. A banner Match Day had hidden is shown again first, with a note saying so.

The last banner can now be deleted; before, the view always wanted one. The "Your banner" camera is only offered when there is a banner. "Show it in the stadium", which puts the stadium beside the artboard, is renamed "Split with the stadium", which is what it does and what the view switcher calls it.

On a phone, "Show in stadium" closes the sheet and the view pill follows it; the pill used to follow only its own buttons. The camera now also fits the banner's width on a screen taller than it is wide. The distance that framed a 41 m banner on a laptop cut off both ends on a phone held upright. Match Day's "Look at it" uses the same camera.

THE HEADER ON LAPTOPS

At 1366 x 768, the most common laptop screen, the header needed 1,369 px as soon as "Saved in this browser" appeared, which happens the moment anything is drawn. Sign up folded into a two-line circle, and Gallery and the account menu went off the edge of the page. Below 1400 px, Community and Gallery move into the account menu, which already has both. Below 1200 px the title narrows and the saved note goes.
