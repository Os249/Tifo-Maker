Banners: a fourth view, a real rig in the bowl, and the reveal

A banner is the half of a tifo the card mosaic isn't, and the app had a modal
with a brush for it. This replaces that with a whole surface.

Banner joins Design / Stadium / Split. It borrows the tool rail and the
palette unchanged — the nine tools keep their icons, their keys and their
tool bars, and reach the artboard through one small object (ui/bannerHost)
rather than by teaching toolbar.ts what a banner is.

A banner is a display list, not a bitmap: polyline strokes, live text, shapes
and images, in units normalised to the banner's width. That is what "draw on
it properly, not pixel art" means, it stays crisp from a 900px artboard to a
print panel, and it serialises to a few KB so banners fit in the save payload.

The research is in the model, not in the copy:
- Überziehfahne vs Aufziehfahne is a property of the type, because one ends up
  ON the crowd (hiding the mosaic) and the other in front of them. Eight types
  in all, each with its own rig and reveal.
- No printer makes fabric wider than 3 m, so the artboard draws the seams.
- Weight is area x g/m²; verify holds the published 70x120m / 756kg flag.
- Mesh is really perforated and alpha-tested, and visibly moves less in wind.

In the bowl, standFrame turns a stand into a surface you can hang things on
(built from the seat map, so it fits any template) and bannerRig builds the
fabric plus the ropes, the net, the weight bar, the poles and the roll. Every
reveal is one 0..1 progress fed to the same surface function, so it can be
scrubbed, previewed, put on the choreography timeline and screenshotted.
Dragging works on the banner itself; the magnet catches the stand centre, the
tier tops, section edges and the halfway line, with a threshold in screen
pixels and a guide that names itself.

Two fixes the screenshots could not have found: the stand window was
s*0.25+0.125 (the END of a stand), so every stand read as empty and every
banner hung on a fallback wall; and hanging a sheet vertically from mid-stand
buries it in the terracing, so stand-mounted banners now follow the stand's
own surface and stand off it along its normal.

Banners save with the design, in their own design_scenes table rather than a
column on designs — the hot save path must not gain a new way to fail. Scenes
are capped at 3 MB gzipped and parsed on the way in.

Phones get a third segment on the view pill and the banner controls in the
existing bottom sheet; the header's segmented control is retuned for four
items at 768px. The pill's buttons now carry data-view: e2e-mobile selected
them by :nth-child, and inserting Banner silently retargeted six checks.

Arabic complete: 81 strings, whole sentences with named placeholders, gated.

Gates: 9 banner gates in verify; npm run shots:banner boots the real simulator
and reads a scene census back (a screenshot cannot tell you the rig group is
empty); e2e-banner.mjs is 47 checks across desktop, two phone orientations,
768x1024 and Arabic RTL, checking pixels and the stored display list rather
than asking the DOM whether drawing happened.

Retires src/ui/bannerStudio.ts; anything it placed is migrated on load.
