## Post it — 5 steps

1. **Swap the sound.** Upload the clip, **mute the original audio**, and add a **trending in-app sound** (Khaleeji/hype or a football trend). TikTok pushes trending audio harder than baked music. The music in the file is only a fallback.
2. **Caption:** `صمّم تيفو ناديك بموقع مجاني 🔥 وش ناديك؟ 👇 tifomaker.org`
3. **Hashtags:** `#تيفو #الهلال #الاتحاد #النصر #الأهلي #دوري_روشن #كورة #السعودية #fyp #tifo #ultras`
4. **Pin the first comment:** `وش ناديك؟ 💬` — seeds the club-tribalism comment loop (comments = watch time = reach).
5. **Timing:** post at **KSA peak (~8–11pm AST)**, ideally on a **match day / derby**.

## A/B testing the hook

Post **one hook per day** (or space them a few days apart), keep everything else identical, and watch the **1-second retention** in analytics — that's the metric Video #1 failed. Whichever hook holds past 0:01 best, make that your template. Then turn it into a **series**: one video per big club, one for the national team (`الأخضر`), one per derby.
