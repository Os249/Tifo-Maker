# AI Video Prompt — "Inspired by real footage" (TifoMaker.org)

## The prompt (paste alongside your 2-min screen recording)

Attached is a ~2-minute screen recording of my real web app, **TifoMaker.org** — treat it as the **visual source of truth** for how the product actually looks and works. Study it closely: the exact UI (layout, colours, fonts, buttons, panels, cursor movement) and the full user journey from start to finish.

Create a high-energy **vertical (9:16)** marketing video, **~15–25s**, that recreates my product and its pipeline with **~99% visual fidelity** — it must look and feel like the real app so a viewer believes they're watching TifoMaker itself. Use the recording as reference and inspiration: you may re-animate, smooth, speed-ramp and polish the screens rather than cutting the raw clip 1:1 — but every screen, colour and interaction must match what's in the recording. Do **NOT** invent fake UI, different layouts, or generic AI stadiums that don't appear in my app.

Reproduce my real pipeline **in order**, exactly as shown in the recording:
1. Land on the TifoMaker.org homepage.
2. Click into the editor ("Design Now").
3. Design a tifo using the tools and the AI panel (pick colours, type a prompt, generate).
4. The tifo appears on the seats.
5. Open the 3D **Stadium** view — the design wraps around the real bowl.
6. Open **Match Day** — the tifo comes alive in a packed, cinematic night stadium.

Make the **hero moment** the flat 2D design exploding to life across the 3D match-day stadium — that's the product's magic; keep it exactly as the app shows it.

Wrap it in marketing polish: a 1-second scroll-stopping hook, bold kinetic captions in the upper third, snappy beat-synced cuts and zoom-punches, an energetic trending hype track, and a short confident voiceover. End on a clean CTA card: **"Design yours free — TifoMaker.org"**.

Format: 9:16 vertical, ~15–25s, TikTok/Reels. Brand: TifoMaker.org.
**Golden rule:** it must look ~99% like my real website and follow my real pipeline — polished, not raw.

## Record the 2-min clip like this (so the AI nails it)
- Full-screen browser, high resolution, no bookmarks bar / clutter.
- Move **slowly and deliberately**; pause a beat on each key screen so it can "read" the UI.
- Capture the whole flow once: homepage → Design Now → a tool + one **AI generate** → tifo on the seats → **Stadium 3D** → **Match Day** reveal.
- Show at least **two different tifos** (e.g. a bold colour one and an AI-generated one) so it learns the range.
- Optional: do a fast pass and a slow pass — gives it more reference to work from.
