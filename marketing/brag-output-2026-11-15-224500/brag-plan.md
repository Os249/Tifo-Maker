# /brag plan: Tifo Maker Pro (full workflow, Hyperframes)

Made with the full /brag skill (github.com/latent-spaces/brag): this plan and
`composition-brief.md` are /brag's; the composition in `composition/` is a
Hyperframes project (v0.9.54), checked with `hyperframes check` and rendered
with `hyperframes render --quality delivery`.

## The questions

- **What is it?** A website where football fans design a tifo for their club
  and see it in a 3D model of their real stadium.
- **Who is it for?** Fans and supporters' groups, mostly on phones, mostly in
  Arabic.
- **What sets it apart?** The seats are the canvas: the tifo is laid seat by
  seat on the real ground, and Match Day films the stand raising it.
- **The focus of this video:** Pro, which opens in a few days. Making a tifo
  stays free; Pro adds extras.
- **Visual hook:** a dark arena, then the whole end stand turning into a tifo.
- **Real UI and output shown:** the Match Day film, the 4K poster, the mark
  and the credit line on a film frame, a Pro font on the seats, the LED
  boards in Match Day.
- **Tone:** `cinematic`, restrained: big type, hard cuts on the bar.
- **Share caption:** see `share-copy.txt`.

## Format

Landscape 1920x1080, 30 fps, 20.5 s (615 frames). Arabic (Saudi dialect).

## Music

"Heroic Age" by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), CC BY 4.0: the 20.5 s from
0:52 of the track, picked by Osamh from five serious options. 130 BPM, a beat
every 0.46 s (`hyperframes beats`). Every cut is on a beat: short scenes are
4 beats, the film and the credit scenes 6. Beat-locked: 1.915 s (the second
hook line), 3.755 s (Pro), 16.685 s (the outro). No sound effects. The gold
glow behind the crown follows the music's loudness.

**Credit required wherever the video is posted:**
"Music: Kevin MacLeod, incompetech.com (CC BY 4.0)".

## Notes

- The tifos are showcase designs made for the video and rendered by the
  site's own Match Day, film and poster code. No user data is shown; the
  credit line "ألتراس الموج الأزرق" is an invented group.
- Two perks have no scene of their own: the club kit (its colours and crest
  are on the poster and the boards) and the supporter crown.
