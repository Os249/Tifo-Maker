# TikTok Video #2 — The Fix Plan

## 1) What the data actually told us

From the analytics of video #1:

- **246 views, 88% from "For You."** The algorithm *did* distribute it — it gave the video a fair test.
- **Average watch 4.1s of 14.4s (~28%); only 4.6% finished; retention cliff at 0:01.** More than half the audience was gone in the first second.
- **Audience: 71% male, 18–34, 69.8% Saudi** + UAE, Egypt, Qatar, Algeria, Kuwait, Iraq — an Arab/Gulf football crowd, on phones.

**Verdict:** distribution wasn't the problem — the **first second** was. And the video spoke **English** to an **Arabic‑first** audience. Three root causes:

1. **Weak/slow hook** — nothing forced them to stay past 0:01.
2. **Wrong language** — English for a 70% Saudi audience.
3. **Reads like an ad**, not native, local content.

Watch time in the first seconds is the single strongest ranking signal on TikTok, and viewers decide to stay or scroll in ~1.7s. Videos that hold 70–85% in the first 3s get ~2.2× more views. That's the whole game, and it's where we lost.

## 2) The three fixes

1. **A hook that lands in under 1 second** — visual interrupt + curiosity + local relevance, all before 0:01.
2. **Arabic‑first** — captions *and* voiceover, Saudi‑friendly.
3. **Native + local** — build the whole thing around **Saudi clubs**.

## 3) The concept: "صمّم تيفو ناديك" (Design your club's tifo)

Your three real clips already look like the three biggest Saudi clubs — use it:

- **Blue → الهلال (Al Hilal)**
- **Gold/black → الاتحاد (Al Ittihad)** *(or النصر — pick per clip)*
- **Green → الأهلي (Al Ahli)**

This is the unlock: naming the clubs turns passive viewers into **commenters** ("وش ناديك؟" / defend your club). Comments = watch time + engagement = more reach. Football‑club tribalism is the most reliable engagement engine in this market.

## 4) Structure (target ~9–11s, loopable)

| Time | What's on screen | Arabic text (gloss) |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–0.8s | **HOOK** — smash‑cut straight into the biggest pyro reveal, no build‑up | `هذا مو ستاد حقيقي 🤯` (*This isn't a real stadium*) |
| 0.8–1.2s | Answer the curiosity | `موقع مجاني تصمّم فيه تيفو ناديك` (*A free site to design your club's tifo*) |
| 1.2–6.5s | Rapid club montage, beat cuts | `الهلال 💙` → `الاتحاد 💛` → `الأهلي 💚` |
| 6.5–8.5s | 3D payoff | `شوفه يشتعل في الملعب ثلاثي الأبعاد` (*Watch it light up in a 3D stadium*) |
| 8.5–11s | CTA + engagement bait | `صمّم تيفو ناديك مجاناً — tifomaker.org` + `وش ناديك؟ 👇` |

End on a frame that cuts back to the hook feel so it **loops**.

## 5) Hook variants to A/B (cut 2–3, test)

- **A) Pattern interrupt:** `هذا مو ستاد حقيقي 👀` over the pyro reveal.
- **B) Relatable + mobile:** `سويت تيفو ناديك من جوّالي 🤯` (*I made my club's tifo from my phone*).
- **C) Aspiration + club bait:** `لو ناديك يطلع بتيفو كذا؟ 🔥` (*What if your club had a tifo like this?*).

Rule: the **wow visual** and the **promise** both happen before 0:01. Lead with the payoff, not a slow intro.

## 6) Voiceover (Arabic) — energetic, ~9s

> `تيفو ملعب… صمّمته من جوّالك. أي نادي، أي لون، أي تصميم. شوفه يشتعل في الملعب ثلاثي الأبعاد. تيفو ميكر — صمّم تيفو ناديك، مجاناً.`

*(Gloss: "A stadium tifo… designed from your phone. Any club, any colour, any design. Watch it light up in a 3D stadium. TifoMaker — design your club's tifo, free.")*

Use a **CapCut Pro Arabic** voice (energetic male). Keep it tight and hype.

## 7) On‑screen text rules

- **Arabic, RTL, BIG, upper‑third.** TikTok's UI (caption, buttons) sits bottom‑right — keep text high so nothing is covered.
- **One short line at a time**, synced to the VO.
- **Club names as their own punchy caption** with a heart emoji in the club colour.

## 8) Sound

Use a **trending sound added in‑app** (Khaleeji/Arab hype or a football/phonk trend), *not* baked music — TikTok's algorithm favours trending audio. In the TikTok editor, mute the clip's audio and drop a trending sound over it. (Keep the composed‑music version as a fallback.)

## 9) Caption + hashtags (post text)

**Caption:** `صمّم تيفو ناديك بموقع مجاني 🔥 وش ناديك؟ 👇 tifomaker.org`

**Hashtags:** `#تيفو #الهلال #الاتحاد #النصر #الأهلي #دوري_روشن #كورة #السعودية #fyp #tifo #ultras`

Rotate the club tags; add the derby/round tag (e.g. `#الكلاسيكو`) when relevant.

## 10) Posting strategy

- **Post at KSA peak** (~8–11pm AST) and around **match days / derbies**.
- **Pin a first comment**: `وش ناديك؟ 💬` to seed the engagement loop.
- **Make it a series** — one video per big club, one for the national team (`الأخضر`), one for a derby. Series = repeat viewers, and each one is a fresh algorithm roll.
- **Reply to comments with clips** — each video‑reply is a new post that rides the original's momentum.

## 11) Also fix

- **Shorter (≤12s)** → higher completion, which is the metric you're weakest on.
- **Loopable** ending.
- **Show the product in use** (design → 3D) to *answer* the curiosity the hook creates.
