## THE PROMPT (copy–paste)

Create a high-energy, cinematic vertical (9:16) social-media marketing video, about 15–20 seconds, for **TifoMaker.org** — a free web app where football fans and ultras design massive 3D stadium tifo choreographies (the giant coordinated card, banner and flag displays fans raise in the stands) and watch them come alive on a realistic 3D match-day stadium.

Goal: stop the scroll in the first second and make football fans desperate to try it. Platform: TikTok / Reels / Shorts.

Audience: passionate football supporters and ultras — young, tribal, obsessed with their club's colours and match-day atmosphere.

Vibe: epic, goosebumps, stadium-at-night — floodlights, smoke, a roaring crowd and a sea of colour across the stands. A Champions-League-anthem moment meets a slick app reveal.

Suggested arc (reinterpret freely — be creative):
- **Hook (first ~1s):** a jaw-dropping full-stadium tifo reveal with a bold line like "This isn't real… yet." or "Design your stadium's tifo."
- Every ultras group dreams of an unforgettable tifo.
- Show how easy it is: a few taps on a phone — pick colours, add text or a crest, let the AI design it.
- **Payoff:** the design explodes to life across a packed 3D stadium at night, lights and crowd reacting.
- Flash the range: a few wildly different tifos — bold colour blocks, a giant portrait, a huge slogan spanning the whole bowl.
- **End card / CTA:** "Design yours free — TifoMaker.org".

Style: dynamic camera moves, punchy beat-synced transitions, light leaks, quick zoom-punches, bold modern kinetic captions in the upper third, high-contrast colour grade. Add an energetic trending-style hype track (epic / phonk build with a drop) and a short, confident voiceover.

Keep the actual product — the 3D stadium tifo — as the hero of every shot, and make it feel like something a real football fan would repost. If I've uploaded my own clips/screenshots, build the edit around them; otherwise generate matching cinematic stadium footage. Be creative with the visuals, transitions, effects and music.

Brand: TifoMaker.org. Keywords: tifo, ultras, choreography, curva, stadium, match-day, football/soccer, fan display.
