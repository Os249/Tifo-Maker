        How it works

        • One tifo each. It has to be published, and made after the event was announced.
        • You can change your entry until entries close.
        • One vote per tifo, with a verified email. Vote for as many as you like, but not for your own.
        • Votes stay hidden until the result, and everyone sees the tifos in a different order.
        • Entering and voting are free. We check the result ourselves before it goes up.
