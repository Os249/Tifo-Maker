PART 2 — WHAT TO CHECK
----------------------

1. Make a sign
   - Open the Banner view and press "Sign" (in the empty card or the add row).
   - The Message field is focused. Type something long, e.g.
     IF YOU CAN'T SEE WHO IS THE LEADER EAT MORE CARROTS
     The words stay fitted to the sheet as you type.
   - Change Length to 25 m and the words refit. Change Height to 1.6 m.
   - Press Enter to leave the field, then Ctrl+Z: the whole message goes
     back in one step, not letter by letter.
   - Font and colour next to the message change it.

2. Place it
   - "Held at": Tied to the front fence, Row 1 — the front, Row 2 ... the
     back. Pick Row 10.
   - "Along": The middle of the stand, In front of block n, or Between
     blocks n and n+1.
   - The facts line says "held by N fans". On the fence it says it is tied on.
   - Press "Show in stadium" on it in the list: the camera looks at it from
     across the pitch, held up over the heads of row 9.
   - Pick Tier 2 and "Tied to the front of the tier": it hangs off the upper
     tier's balcony.

3. Match Day
   - "See it on match day": it is raised by the crowd.
   - In the Match Day banner panel, a sign has "Held at" and "Along" where
     other banners have Across, Blocks and Span.
   - Click it once, then drag it: it moves to the row and place under the
     pointer.

4. Arabic
   - Switch to Arabic: the type is "يافطة", every field is translated, and an
     Arabic message reads right to left on the sheet.

5. The community
   - /community has a fourth card, "Red & white halves, with signs". Open it:
     the preview shows three signs, one of them in Arabic on the balcony.


PART 3 — COMMIT
---------------

1. Stop both (Ctrl+C in each window).

2. Still to do from before: delete the five retired banner files. They are
   empty placeholders, so nothing breaks while they are there, but they
   should go:

       Remove-Item scripts\banner-physics.mts
       Remove-Item src\core\bannerMigrate.ts
       Remove-Item src\render\simulator\bannerFit.ts
       Remove-Item src\render\simulator\cloth.ts
       Remove-Item src\render\simulator\standHeightfield.ts

   Optional, the same kind of leftover:

       Remove-Item src\ui\bannerStudio.ts

   There is also a stray, empty folder named "{core,render,ui}" inside src.
   Delete it too (the quotes matter, because of the braces):

       Remove-Item -LiteralPath 'src\{core,render,ui}'

3. Prove it builds:

       npm run build
       npm run verify

4. Commit. The message is in BANNERS-REBUILD-COMMIT.txt in this folder, with
   the Summary and the Description labelled separately. Paste them into
   GitHub Desktop's two fields.

5. Pushing is yours. Pushing main runs CI, and Railway deploys main, so push
   after step 3 passes. On the deploy, the server adds the signs showcase by
   itself.
